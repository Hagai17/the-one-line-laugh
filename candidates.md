# Candidates — The One-Line Laugh

Thirteen candidate one-liners were drafted, counted exactly by script, and duelled pairwise on the judge's criteria in order; the round-1 winner then defended its place against three fresh challengers in a revision round.

**Final winner (87 chars):** I've had a gym bag packed by the door since January; it is now the fittest thing I own.

**Runner-up (107 chars):** My car has been making a strange noise for six months, and I've fixed it every day by turning the radio up.

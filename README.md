A tiny Python program that writes one funny line to `/output/entry.txt`: a single line of at most 200 characters, on any subject, built to land a real surprise at the end. The line was picked by drafting ten candidates plus three revision challengers and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — one funny line, a turn at the end the reader did not see coming, an everyday truth people recognise, every word earning its place, and fresh rather than a line heard many times before. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
